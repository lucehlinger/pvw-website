FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an Oura ring or other wearable?

No. Plenty of clients don't. If you do wear an Oura, Whoop, or Fitbit, Apex and Director bring that data into your coaching.

Is coaching in person or online?

Both. Calls are virtual by default, and Director clients get quarterly in-person assessments in Austin.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans cancel with 24 hours' notice and you keep access through the end of your billing period. Packages (3, 6, or 12 months) run for their full term.

Can I upgrade in the middle of a package?

Yes. If you start on Toolkit and want more support, move up to Apex anytime. Whatever is left on your package is credited toward the upgrade.

What happens on the free call?

45 minutes on your goals, your routine, and where you're stuck. If it's a fit, we'll pick the right plan together.
