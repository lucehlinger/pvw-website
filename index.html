Hi, I'm Luc.

This work is personal for me. I've dealt with autoimmune conditions and food sensitivities of my own, and for years I was guessing at what my body actually needed.

My Oura ring showed me what was affecting my sleep, and tracking my food and activity helped me connect how I felt to what I was eating and doing. But knowing wasn't enough to stay consistent. What actually made the difference was accountability: people around me who expected me to follow through.

Looking back, having a coach in my corner would have gotten me there years sooner. Now I get to be that person for others, with protocols grounded in solid research, fit to your life, and someone checking in to keep you on track.

Common questions

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice changes in sleep and energy within the first few weeks. Body composition takes longer, usually a few months of consistent work, which is why packages start at 3 months.

Are your recommendations based on research?

Yes. Your protocol draws on well-supported research in nutrition, sleep, and behavior change. Then we adjust it based on how your body actually responds.

What if I already have the basics down?

Then we skip ahead. If your sleep, nutrition, and habits are already solid, we focus on optimizing: recovery, body composition, training results, and supplement strategy.

Do I need an Oura ring or other wearable?

No. Plenty of clients don't. If you do wear an Oura, Whoop, or Fitbit, Apex and Director bring that data into your coaching.

Is coaching in person or online?

Both. Calls are virtual by default, and Director clients get quarterly in-person assessments in Austin.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans cancel with 24 hours' notice and you keep access through the end of your billing period. Packages (3, 6, or 12 months) run for their full term.

Can I upgrade in the middle of a package?

Yes. If you start on Toolkit and want more support, move up to Apex anytime. Whatever is left on your package is credited toward the upgrade.

What happens on the free call?

45 minutes on your goals, your routine, and where you're stuck. If it's a fit, we'll pick the right plan together.
